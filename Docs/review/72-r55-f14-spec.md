# 72 — R55-F14 spec: relay-driven audit and log volume

Ticket R55-F14 (review 55 [99-report.md](55-code-review/99-report.md) §4, §4.1): R55-015,
R55-016, R55-042. Owner decision **D49** (HANDOFF §3, review 55 D10). Spec only; code comes
after F9 and F2 (see §7). Base: main `f2e3415`.

## 1. Summary

A hostile relay can make the daemon write without bound to two places:

- **The audit log.** It is append-only and never pruned. Any frame from an unpaired key gives
  one `mail.reject` or `session.reject` row, 30 of each a minute, about 29 MB a day for ever
  (R55-015, T10-01).
- **The daemon log.** Every reject and every bad relay frame writes one line, before any
  limiter (R55-042, R55-016). On macOS that log is launchd's unrotated stderr file, so it can
  grow by GBs an hour.

D49: rejects of frames from unpaired keys are not audited and go to a counted, rate-limited
log line. The audit log stays append-only. The macOS plist passes `--log-file`.

**A gap in D49 as worded (OD-F14-1).** The relay can put any `from` on a frame, including a
paired peer's key, which it knows because it routes that peer's traffic. Such a frame passes
step 1 (`unpaired`) and fails at step 2–7 (mail) or anywhere in the session layer. That is
still audited, at the same 30+30 rows a minute. It can also replay any envelope it carried.
An old mail replayed after `mail_seen` has forgotten it gives `stale`, and a replayed
`session.data` gives `replay`. So D49 taken literally does not close R55-015 against its own
actor. The spec therefore applies D49's reasoning to **every reject that a relay alone can
cause** (OD-F14-1 (b), recommended). The audit keeps only rejects of mail that the peer
verifiably signed (steps 8, 9, 10, 12 and `bad_body`; not a step-12 reject for an expired
`keys` announcement, which a replay causes, review 72b). Each is audited once per
`(peer, id)`, so replaying one rejected envelope gives one row. No session reject is audited
any more, because every session reason can be forged.

The fix, in five rules:

1. **Audit only what a relay cannot cause.** Mail rejects at steps 1–7, every `stale`, and a
   step-12 `bad_keys` for an expired announcement are logged only. Steps 8, 9, 10 and 12
   (other cases) and `bad_body` are audited once per `(peer, id)` per run, still at most 30
   a minute. No new `session.reject` rows. If the owner picks OD-F14-7 (b), logged-only
   rejects except `unpaired` are counted into one daily `relay.reject_summary` row.
2. **`id` only when valid.** A `mail.reject` detail carries `id` only if it matches the
   mail id format; otherwise `{peer, reason}`.
3. **`mail.in` of an unknown kind is audited as `kind: "unknown"`.** No kind may be
   registered under that name.
4. **One log line a minute per relay-driven event, as a count** (review 72b: written at the
   end of the minute with `count`, per-reason `reasons` and the first occurrence's fields;
   flushed at stop). The limiter is keyed by the fixed event name. It covers
   `relay_bad_frame` (F2, unchanged), `relay_misrouted` and `relay_error_frame` (F9),
   `relay_ack_failed`, `mail_reject`, `mail_ack_failed`, `session_reject`, `session_drop`
   and `session_send_failed`.
5. **macOS rotates.** The plist passes `--log-file <home>/agentnetd.log`; launchd's
   stdout/stderr go to a separate `<home>/agentnetd.out.log`, which the daemon renames to
   `.1` at start when it is over 1 MiB (OD-F14-3 (e), review 72b).

**Wire change:** none. **Migration:** none. Old `mail.reject`/`session.reject` rows stay; the
chain is untouched.

## 3. Design notes

- **Why a reject from step 8 on is the peer's.** Step 7 verifies the peer's signature over
  `msg`. Steps 8 (`to`), 9 (`id`), 10 (`v`) and 12 (body of `ack`/`keys`) then judge content
  that the peer signed. Step 4 binds the envelope `to`, `from` and `id` into the HPKE open
  (info and aad), so the relay cannot re-address or re-id a genuine mail either: it fails
  at step 3 or 4. A relay cannot make one; it can only replay one it carried. The
  `(peer, id)` set makes each replay after the first free. Review 72b found one
  time-dependent step-12 check: `keys` announcements expire (`not_after`), `keys` has no
  receive age limit and step 12 runs before dedupe, so a replayed genuine `keys` mail can
  fail step 12 without any fault of the peer. That case is logged only. `stale` is the exception: it is
  also post-signature, but the relay holds a copy of **every** mail it carried and can replay
  them once they are older than 30 days (`mail_seen` is pruned at 35 d). So `stale` is logged
  only. The receive-age `stale` (> 14 d) comes before dedupe, so the same holds.
- **Why every session reject.** Noise message 1 needs no secret, so the relay can start a
  handshake with a paired peer's key as `from`. The daemon answers with `resp`, and a forged
  `fin` then fails with `bad_binding` or `bad_handshake`. `decrypt`, `unknown_session` and
  `malformed` need only garbage; `replay` needs one captured frame. No session reason proves
  the peer's involvement. `session.open` is unchanged, because it needs the peer's `fin`
  (initiator: its `resp`) to verify.
- **Dedupe set bounds.** 4096 entries of `peer‖id` (≤ ~180 B each, < 1 MiB), FIFO eviction,
  in memory, per run. To cycle it, a relay needs more than 4096 distinct genuine rejected
  envelopes. A peer produces those only by sending bad mail, which is peer-driven growth
  (R55-F13), not relay-driven.
- **Limiter keyed by event, counts by reason.** A per-peer key needs a Sybil-bounded map
  (compare presence's `maxDropPeers`); a line per `(event, reason)` gives about 25 lines a
  minute under a mixed flood. The event key gives at most nine lines a minute and a
  constant map. Review 72b added the per-reason counts inside the one line, because with a
  first-occurrence line alone the relay picks what is shown (it sends one `unpaired` frame
  at the start of each minute) and a `bad_signature` under a paired peer's `from` in the
  same minute would never appear (OD-F14-2).
- **Trailing count, not leading line (review 72b).** The first draft wrote the first
  occurrence at once and its count only with the next line. A flood that then stopped left
  its size in memory, lost at the next stop, so the log showed one line for 10 000 forged
  frames. The line is now written at the end of the window and at stop, as F2's
  `relay_bad_frame` already does. Cost: a single event appears up to a minute late.
- **Limiter counts at every level.** Counting is independent of the handler's level, so
  running at Debug does not change the count. `relay_misrouted` and `relay_error_frame` are
  Debug and still limited.
- **Separate `agentnetd.out.log`.** launchd opens `StandardErrorPath` itself. If it were the
  `--log-file` path, `logfile` would rename it to `.1` and later delete it, and stderr (crash
  reports) would land in a deleted file. The size counter would also miss launchd's writes.
- **The `id` in log lines.** `mail_reject` and `session_reject` no longer log the envelope id.
  It is relay-chosen and not needed to diagnose a flood. `relay_misrouted` and
  `relay_ack_failed` keep it: F9 chose that, it is Validate-bounded, and now limited.

## 4. Plan (files and functions)

1. **`internal/lograte/lograte.go` (new).**
   `func New(log *slog.Logger, window time.Duration) *Limiter` (0 → 1 min; `after` and
   `now` replaceable in tests). `func (l *Limiter) Note(level slog.Level, event, msg,
   reason string, first ...any)`: counts one occurrence; the first in a window stores
   `level`, `msg` and `first` and schedules the flush (`time.AfterFunc`, as F2's
   `badFrames`); `reason` (may be `""`) is counted in a per-event map. The flush writes
   `msg` with `event`, `count`, `reasons` (sorted `k=v` string, omitted when empty) and
   `first`, then clears the event. `func (l *Limiter) Flush()` writes every pending line
   (daemon stop; `relayclient` at connection end). Doc comment: `event` and `reason` must be
   constants, never input. Tests: fake clock/after, count, reasons, window roll, flush at
   stop, map size = distinct events.
2. **`internal/mail/audit.go`.**
   - `Report(peer, id, reason string)` becomes `Report(peer, id string, err error)`; `err` is
     a `*RejectError` (the receiver passes `reject(11, ReasonStale, nil)` and
     `reject(11, ReasonBadBody, nil)`).
   - Always: `a.lines.Note(slog.LevelInfo, "mail_reject", "mail envelopes rejected",
     re.Reason, "reason", re.Reason, "step", re.Step, "peer", peer)`.
   - `auditable(re) = re.Reason == ReasonBadBody || (re.Step >= 8 && re.Step <= 12 &&
     re.Step != 11 && !(re.Step == 12 && errors.Is(re.Err, ErrAnnouncementExpired)))`.
     If not auditable: count into the summary (OD-F14-7 (b); not for `unpaired`), return.
   - Dedupe: `seen` set (map plus FIFO ring of 4096 `peer+"\x00"+id`); already present →
     return. Then the existing 30/min budget and `mail_reject_suppressed`.
   - Detail: `{"peer", "reason"}` plus `"id"` only if `ValidID(id)`.
3. **`internal/mail/open.go`.** `Open` passes `err` to `Report`. Check that
   `checkKeysBody`'s wrapping keeps `ErrAnnouncementExpired` reachable by `errors.Is`
   (`%w: %w`; it does today).
4. **`internal/mail/receiver.go` `Handle` and `ack`.**
   - The receive-age `stale` and `bad_body` reports pass a `*RejectError` (above).
   - `mail.in`: `auditKind := kind`; if `!known`, `auditKind = "unknown"`.
   - `ack`: the three Warns (`ack_no_mailbox_key`, `ack_seal_failed`, `ack_send_failed`)
     become one limited event `mail_ack_failed` with reasons `no_mailbox_key`,
     `seal_failed`, `send_failed`. A relay replaying a genuine mail makes one ack attempt
     per replay, so these were per-frame.
5. **`internal/session/session.go`.**
   - `reject`: no `Audit.Append`. `lines.Note(Info, "session_reject", …, reason, "reason",
     reason, "type", e.Type, "peer", e.From, "session"?)`; count into the summary unless
     `unpaired`.
   - Remove `rejMu`, `rejWindow`, `rejCount`, `suppressed`, `rejectsPerMinute`, and
     `ActionReject` (its tests move to log assertions).
   - `HandleEnvelope`: `session_drop` via the limiter, without `id`. `send`: the
     `session_send_failed` Warn via the limiter (a forged `init` makes the daemon send a
     `resp`; a relay that stops reading makes each send fail). `Stop` calls `lines.Flush()`.
6. **`internal/relayclient/relayclient.go` `dispatch`** (after F9 and F2).
   - One `lines *lograte.Limiter` per `Client`, flushed where F2 flushes `badFrames`.
   - `relay_misrouted`, `relay_error_frame` (F9's Debug lines) and `relay_ack_failed` go
     through it.
   - F2's `badFrames` already meets the rule (trailing count per minute, flushed at
     connection end): leave it. Moving it onto `lograte` is optional and must keep its
     output (`count`, `acked`).
7. **`internal/service/launchd.go` `LaunchdPlist`.**
   - After the relay args, add `<string>--log-file</string><string>` + `xmlEscape(path.Join(spec.Home,
     LogFileName))` + `</string>` (same order as the Windows task).
   - `StandardOutPath`/`StandardErrorPath` → `path.Join(spec.Home, LaunchdOutFileName)`, a new
     const `"agentnetd.out.log"`.
8. **`cmd/agentnetd/main.go` (OD-F14-3 (e)).** Before anything is written to stderr: if
   `os.Stderr.Stat()` is a regular file, `os.SameFile` with `<home>/agentnetd.out.log`, and
   its size is over `logfile.MaxSize`, remove `agentnetd.out.log.1` and rename the file to
   it. Errors are ignored (best effort). No other platform uses that name.
9. **Summary row (OD-F14-7 (b)).** A small `internal/daemon` type holds the counts
   `map[layer]map[reason]int` (fixed keys) behind a mutex, fed by mail `RejectAudit` and the
   session manager; a 24 h ticker and the daemon's clean stop write `relay.reject_summary
   {since, until, mail, session}` when non-zero, actor `daemon`. List the action in
   audit.md and cover it in the no-content test.
10. **Docs** (done in this spec): see §8.

### Findings

1. **High: the limiter let the relay choose what the log shows, and it lost counts.**
   A line described only the first occurrence of the minute. A relay that sends one
   `unpaired` frame at the start of each minute therefore hides every `bad_signature` under
   a paired peer's `from` in that minute; the only trace was a total in the next line. A
   flood that stopped left its `suppressed_before` in memory until the next event, or lost
   it at a stop. **Changed:** one line at the end of the window, and at stop or connection
   end. It holds `count`, per-reason `reasons` (reasons are code constants, so the map stays
   bounded) and the first occurrence's fields. This is F2's `relay_bad_frame` shape
   (envelope.md §Relay-driven log lines, mail.md, session.md, spec §1 rule 4, §3, plan 1,
   test 5, OD-F14-2). Residual: the peer of a hidden occurrence is not named (Sybil). See
   finding 4.
2. **Medium: some per-frame lines that a relay can drive were not limited.** These were F9's
   `relay_error_frame` (a Debug line per error frame), the receiver's `ack_no_mailbox_key`,
   `ack_seal_failed` and `ack_send_failed` (one per replayed genuine mail), and
   `session_send_failed` (a forged `init` makes the daemon send a `resp`; a relay that stops
   reading makes each send fail). **Changed:** added to the table as `relay_error_frame`,
   `mail_ack_failed` (with reasons) and `session_send_failed`, now 9 events. The worst case
   is ≈ 3 MB a day (plan 4–6).
3. **Medium: a step-12 `bad_keys` for an expired announcement was audited, but a relay
   replay causes it.** **Changed:** it is log only (mail.md, audit.md table, spec §1, §3,
   `auditable` in plan 2 using `errors.Is(re.Err, ErrAnnouncementExpired)`, test 1).
   `Report` now takes the `*RejectError`.
4. **Medium: nothing lasting remained of the relay-causable rejects.** Evidence of a hostile
   relay would be forged `bad_signature`/`sender_mismatch`/`decrypt` under a paired `from`,
   or session `bad_binding`. That evidence now lives only in a 2 MiB rotated log, which a
   relay can flush within hours. **Changed:** added OD-F14-7. The recommended (b) is one
   `relay.reject_summary` row a day and at a clean stop, written only when non-zero. It has
   fixed `(layer, reason)` keys and integer counts, excludes `unpaired` (D49) and names no
   peer. That is ≤ 365 rows a year, and a relay can change the numbers but not the row
   count (audit.md "Daily summary", mail.md, session.md, plan 9, tests 1 and 9).
5. **Medium: `agentnetd.out.log` is unbounded if a relay can trigger a panic.** With a crash
   loop every 10 s, each crash writes a Go crash report of tens of KB. **Changed:** added
   OD-F14-3 (e), recommended. At start the daemon renames the file to `.1` when its stderr
   is that file and it is over 1 MiB; launchd reopens the path at the next start, so the
   latest crash report is kept. The implementer confirms launchd's reopen-per-start once on
   a Mac. This is harmless if it is wrong: only the bound is lost. I also corrected "launchd
   holds its own descriptor": the daemon's fd 1 and fd 2 are the descriptors (agentnetd-install.md, plan 8,
   test 8).
6. **Low: the F2 row did not match F2.** F2's `relay_bad_frame` is a trailing
   `count`/`acked` line per minute, flushed at connection end, with no `suppressed_before`.
   **Changed:** the table row and plan 6 (leave F2 as it is).
7. **Low: tests that break were missing.**
   - `mail_test.go` `TestRejectAuditRateLimit` uses unpaired rejects.
   - `service_test.go:194` expects `--relay` right before `</array>`.
   - T10-01 lives as `Docs/review/55-code-review/tests/zz_review55_T10-01_test.go.txt`.

   **Changed:** tests 1, 2 and 7. `TestAuditInventory` is really unaffected, because it
   lists methods and kinds, not reject actions.
8. **Low: two other docs were stale.** request.md §Invalid bodies still said "like other
   rejects", and the work-session.md naming note still listed `session.reject`.
   **Changed:** both.
9. **Low: the dedupe residual was not stated.** A restart empties the 4096-entry set, so
   each genuine rejected envelope can be re-audited once per run (mail.md, OD-F14-5).
10. **Low: existing macOS installs.** The new binary's limiter already bounds an old,
    unrotated plist to a few MB a day. Also, the old large log sits in `agentnetd.log.1`
    until the next rotation, which can take weeks at a normal rate, so it can be deleted by
    hand (agentnetd-install.md).

Not changed, after checking:
- Session rejects are all relay-causable, and `session.open` still needs the peer.
- `OpenPresence` never audits.
- Key-miss recovery (`KeyMiss.Note`) is per-peer windowed and writes no audit row.
- The `mail.in` `unknown` kind is fine.
- Connection lines stay bounded by the backoff (OD-F14-6).

### Final owner decisions and how they differ from D49's wording

D49 says three things. Unpaired rejects are not audited (a counted, rate-limited log line
only). The audit log stays append-only. The macOS plist passes `--log-file`.
