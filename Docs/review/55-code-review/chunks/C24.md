# C24: Debates: schemas, decode/validate, constraints, view, decision derivation

Reviewer: CR-C24 · model claude-opus-5-5 · elapsed 5 min · commit 6cc26a7

## Summary

| Critical | High | Medium | Low | Info |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Verdict: the entry schemas, the strict decoders and the debate-text rules match debate.md
§Messages. The constraint visibility rule matches review 46 H1. Nothing reaches a debate
table before approval. `debate_constrain`'s Perform writes only through its transaction. I
found no input a modified peer controls that makes the honest side sign a Decision its own
transcript contradicts. There is one real gap. B takes the `constraints` list of A's close
unchecked: no dedupe, no bound. A modified A can repeat one id until B's derivation goes over
`MaxDecision`. The close then fails with a plain error, is never acked and becomes a poison
mail (C24-01, confirmed by test). The other two findings are a Low spec deviation (constraints
accepted in `invited`) and an Info note.

## Questions

1. **Decode/validate.** Every entry goes through `DecodeEntry` (`internal/debate/decode.go:20`),
   with exact members via `object` (`decode.go:317`). An optional array must be non-empty
   (`decode.go:346`), an optional string must be non-empty (`nonEmpty`, `decode.go:383`), and
   `null` is refused (`typeErr`). The field caps are checked before `CheckSize` (`decode.go:40-46`).
   Every Line(N) goes through `checkLine` (`text.go:29`): valid UTF-8, a code-point count,
   `badDebateText` (C0, DEL, C1, U+2028/9; `text.go:23`; `request.HasControl` also refuses
   U+FFFD, `internal/request/validate.go:344`), and trimmed. `argument` uses `checkArgument`
   (`text.go:53`). Proposal artifacts get the request rules plus C1 and trim
   (`validate.go:144-173`). The topic's debate-text rule is enforced in the request validator
   (`internal/request/validate.go:119-147`), not by `ValidateTopic`, which only
   `DecisionSchema` uses (`decision.go:48`). The two implementations are equivalent today. The
   constraint rule is in `ValidateConstraintText`/`invisibleRune` (`text.go:75-96`): Cf, VS,
   ODI, Zs other than U+0020, U+2800 and non-graphic are refused, as in the spec (R46 H1).
   Canonical form: both sides store `Canonical(decoded)` (`canonical.go:9`), and stored bytes
   are re-checked by `ParseEntry` (`decode.go:53`). Only the constraint validator depends on the
   Unicode tables (`IsGraphic`, Cf, VS, ODI, Zs). `checkTrimmed` uses `unicode.IsSpace`, which
   is stable in practice. So Go/Unicode skew can only make a receiver refuse a constraint. That
   leads to the silent hold of O-165 (R46 L4). No new path.
2. **Constraints.** `PrepareConstraint` (`constraint.go:94`) only reads. `AddConstraintTx`
   (`constraint.go:127`) runs inside the approval's Perform. The summary shows the full text
   through `device.DisplayQuote` and is bounded by `notify.MaxWindowSummary`
   (`internal/daemon/debate_constrain.go:58,95`). The text still sits in `approvals.summary`
   (O-164). The hold for missing ids (`missingConstraints`, `constraint.go:252`;
   `apply.go:612-624`) is unbounded in time. A modified A can list an id it never sends and
   stall B until B's human abandons: that is O-165 as recorded, not worse. A **new** stall
   variant comes from the list's contents, not missing ids: see C24-01. It also turns a
   `debate.constraint` or entry mail that re-triggers `retryHeldClose` into a poison mail.
3. **Derivation.** `deriveTx` (`decision.go:57`) takes only slots `< entries` in state
   `applied`/`sent`, the listed constraints, and A's outcome, reason and `at`.
   `decision.Derive` re-checks rule 6 (`ErrInconsistent`). So A cannot claim `agreed` and
   also cut B's answer (it gets refused "outcome"), and it cannot fabricate an answer.
   `closeMatches` (`apply.go:694`) runs first, and `closeGap` (`decision.go:187`) refuses cut
   A entries and unsent B slots. A can still drop B's last entry by claiming a timeout: that is
   O-175, accepted. B's constraints are stored before they are sent, and A's are stored with
   author `initiator` (`constraint.go:215`), so A cannot sign a constraint in as B's. What A
   *can* control unchecked is the multiplicity and length of `constraints` (C24-01). With a
   short duplicate list, both sides derive and sign a Decision that holds the same human
   decision twice (`decision.Derive` sorts but does not dedupe,
   `internal/decision/decision.go:319`). Verify does not refuse that either
   (`internal/decision/verify.go:393-422`).
4. **`debate_constrain` IPC.** It is approval-gated (`approval.KindDebateConstraint`,
   `debate_constrain.go:128`). The precondition re-checks the phase and the limit in the
   confirm transaction (`constraint.go:114`). Perform writes the row, the mail
   (`Outbox.SubmitTx(tx)`) and the audit (`auditTx`, ids only) in the approval's tx, and
   `Wake` runs after commit (`debate_constrain.go:109-126`). The id is still passed through a
   mutex-guarded closure set after `Create` (`debate_constrain.go:98-134`): O-147, unchanged.

## Findings

### C24-01 · Medium · confirmed-test
- **Where:** `internal/debate/apply.go:503-511` (close `constraints` only pattern-checked),
  `internal/debate/decision.go:72-84` (`deriveTx` loads each listed id, duplicates included),
  `internal/debate/apply.go:626-628` (non-sentinel `Derive` error returned as-is)
- **What goes wrong:** B accepts a `debate.close` whose `constraints` array repeats ids and
  has no length bound. Each repetition adds one copy of the constraint to the derived
  Decision. Past `MaxDecision`, `decision.Derive` returns `*TooLargeError`, which is neither
  `ErrBadBody` nor a refusal path. The mail transaction rolls back and the close is never
  acked, refused or stored.
- **Scenario:** a paired peer running a modified initiator daemon holds a debate with B (who
  added one constraint, or received one from A). In the close it sends
  `constraints: [c-X × 400]` (≈15 KB of body). B checks the outcome, the entry gap and the
  missing ids (none are missing), then calls `deriveTx` → 841182 bytes > 786432 → error. The
  receiver does not ack (`internal/mail/receiver.go:204-209`), so A's outbox re-sends it for
  up to 7 days and each delivery costs B a full derivation. B's debate stays open with
  `waiting: "signature"`, and nothing is audited or shown. If the close was first held (it
  also lists a missing id), every later A entry or constraint that re-runs
  `retryHeldClose` (`constraint.go:227`, `apply.go` `retryIfHeld`) fails the same way, so
  those mails are poisoned too. With a short list (2 copies), both sides instead sign a
  Decision that holds a duplicated human decision, which the spec's "sorted list of ids"
  rules out. Exit: B's human abandons. `applyClose` then stores the body and acks
  (`apply.go:560-567`).
- **Spec:** `Docs/protocol/decision.md` §Signing step 1, "`constraints` is the sorted list of
  constraint ids A holds"; §Size, "exceeding it is a bug, not a peer error"; debate.md
  §Human constraints, "At most 10 `active` constraints per debate".
- **Fix direction:** in `applyClose`, refuse as `bad_body` a `constraints` list that is not
  strictly sorted/unique or has more than `MaxActiveConstraints` (or `maxStoredConstraints`)
  items. Map `decision.TooLargeError` on B to a refusal instead of a raw error.
- **Related:** O-165 (the same stall exit), O-175; lead for C25 (Verify accepts duplicate
  `human_decisions` ids).

### C24-02 · Low · confirmed-read
- **Where:** `internal/debate/constraint.go:185` (`applyConstraint` phase check)
- **What goes wrong:** a received `debate.constraint` is refused only in
  `closing`/`closed`/`broken`, so one arriving while the debate is `invited` (before the
  accept) is stored `active`. The spec allows constraints only in `positions`, `rounds` or
  `converge`.
- **Scenario:** a modified peer sends up to 10 constraints for a debate it was invited to (B
  modified, A `invited`), or for one it invited to (A modified, B `invited`). The receiver
  stores them active, shows them in `debate_show` of a debate not yet accepted, and they count
  toward the 10-active limit. So the honest side's own later `--constrain` gets
  `constraint_limit` once the debate opens. The texts still appear under the sender's own side
  only.
- **Spec:** `Docs/protocol/debate.md` §Human constraints, "Who … while the debate is in
  `positions`, `rounds` or `converge`".
- **Fix direction:** ignore (`debate.ignored {reason: "state"}`) a constraint whose debate is
  not in one of the three phases.
- **Related:** none

### C24-03 · Info · confirmed-read
- **Where:** `internal/debate/experience.go:50-68` (`proposalDecision`, `remainingDisagreement`)
- **What goes wrong:** these helpers scan every transcript row regardless of state or of A's
  `entries`, unlike `claimOf` (`experience.go:36`). On B, an answer that A cut by claiming a
  timeout (O-175) still feeds `HasRemainingDisagreement` in B's experience record, which
  therefore differs from what the Decision says.
- **Scenario:** a modified A closes `escalated/timeout` with `entries` excluding B's answer.
  B's experience record reports B's answer's disagreement count. Local data only, no security
  effect.
- **Spec:** `Docs/protocol/experience.md` (the record summarises the debate as closed).
- **Fix direction:** restrict both helpers to `applied`/`sent` rows below the close's count.
- **Related:** O-175

## Assumptions and contracts

- `DecodeEntry` assumes its input came from `agentcard.ParseStrict` or the IPC parser
  (duplicate keys refused, strings valid UTF-8). Callers: `apply.go:130` (the peer body is
  parsed by the mail layer), `submit.go:94` and `start.go:70` via
  `internal/daemon/debate.go:308` (`agentcard.ParseStrict`). **Checked** for the IPC side;
  **unchecked** that the mail opener uses ParseStrict for bodies.
- The debate-text rule for the topic relies on `request.Validate`
  (`internal/request/validate.go:119-147`) being run on both submit and receipt of a `debate`
  request. **Checked** in the validator; the call sites are in C21/C22's scope.
- `deriveTx` assumes the stored request `body` is byte-identical canonical JSON on A (`out`)
  and on B (`in`) (`decision.go:63`). **Unchecked** (request storage, C21/C22).
- `ValidateConstraintText` assumes both daemons use the same Unicode tables (Go version).
  **Unchecked**: a skew leads to O-165.
- `debate_constrain` assumes `approval.Store` runs Precondition and Perform in one
  transaction, and keeps the constraint in memory only (`debate_constrain.go:100-128`).
  **Unchecked** (approval chunk).
- `applyCloseOnB` assumes `decision.Derive` returns only `ErrIncomplete`, `ErrInconsistent`,
  `ErrClosedBeforeOpened` for peer-caused problems. **Checked, false**: `*TooLargeError` too
  (C24-01).
- `constraintSummary` relies on `device.DisplayQuote` escaping every non-graphic/Cf rune
  (`internal/device/scope.go:360`). **Checked.**

## Checked and fine

- Strict member sets for position, move, challenge, proposal, agreement, answer, evidence,
  alternative, disagreement and artifact (`decode.go`, `request/decode.go:174`).
- Targets: syntax, no leading zeros, bounded index, distinct, and existence in the other
  side's current position (`validate.go:68-118,241-287`).
- Size: the canonical entry is capped at 32768 after the field caps (`canonical.go:15`).
- The constraint-visible rule covers Cf, VS (both ranges), ODI, non-ASCII Zs and U+2800
  (`text.go:90-96`), and is applied at IPC (`constraint.go:95`), on receipt
  (`constraint.go:168`) and in Verify (`decision.go:49`).
- No constraint text in the audit (`constraint.go:225`, `debate_constrain.go:116`) or in
  events. `Store.List` loads counts only (`view.go:106`, O-166 part fixed).
- The receiver stores at most 20 per debate; A keeps the 11th and later as `excess`, never
  listed or shown (`constraint.go:201-224`, `view.go`/`loadConstraints`).
- `sign` refuses a keystore key that does not match the card (`decision.go:110`).
- `applySign` on A: a hash mismatch or a bad signature becomes `peer_refused`, never
  `signed` (`decision.go:378-397`).
- `decideTx` never sends `closed < opened` (`decision.go:160`).
- The view hides `early` entries and marks B's uncounted entries `late` (`view.go:131-156`).
- IPC error mapping for debates is complete (`internal/daemon/debate.go:227`).
