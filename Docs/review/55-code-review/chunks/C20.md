# C20: Teams: store, kinds, invites

Reviewer: CR-C20 · model claude-opus-5-5 · elapsed 6 min (shell clock 00:50→00:56; see Commands run) · commit 6cc26a7

## Summary

Critical 0 · High 0 · Medium 0 · Low 4 · Info 1.

The trust core of teams holds. Only a peer with trust `code`/`fingerprint` that is `msg.from`
and `team.owner` can apply a roster. Epochs never roll back. Re-entry after
`left`/`removed`/`dissolved`, and entry into an unknown team, both need a live pending join
from that owner. GC only ever deletes `introduced_by IS NOT NULL` rows, after the
teams/members writes in the same transaction. `team.join` and `team.leave` are bounded by
owner, invite and state. What remains are liveness and consistency gaps around leave and
rejoin:
- the R16 M2 "rejoin before the leave arrived" fix is undone when that leave does arrive
  (C20-01, shown by a failing test);
- goodbyes are never sent to the introduced peers a leave collects (C20-02);
- a failed `team.leave` submit cannot be retried (C20-03);
- `team show`/`team join` print peer card names raw (C20-04).

## Questions

1. **Who can introduce; epochs; re-entry.**
   - `applyRoster` requires `team.owner = msg.from` (`internal/team/kinds.go:71`) and the
     owner's current `peers.trust ∈ {code, fingerprint}` (`kinds.go:76-82`). A member, an
     introduced peer (`trust=team`) or a `relay` peer gets `owner_trust`, and the relay
     cannot forge `msg.from`.
   - A known team with a different owner is `not_owner` (`kinds.go:105`), so a peer cannot
     roster a team I own or someone else's team. Squatting is O-065.
   - `epoch ≤ teams.epoch` is ignored silently (`kinds.go:108`), and that check comes before
     the state branch, so a replayed older roster cannot re-enter.
   - Unknown team and non-active local state both need `consumePendingJoin` for `msg.from`
     (`kinds.go:91-121`). It is only attempted when the roster is `active` and lists self
     (R16 fix, `kinds.go:87`). R12 M1 holds. Matching is on the owner only (O-080).
   - Self in `members` is skipped for `Introduce` (`kinds.go:181`). That covers O-045 on
     this path.
2. **Size, verification, GC order, direct peers.**
   - Members are capped at 1–32 (0–32 when dissolved), with unique keys and the owner
     included when active (`kinds.go:304-326`).
   - Each card is canonicalised and `agentcard.Verify`'d, with `public_key == key`
     (`kinds.go:349-359`).
   - Announcements go through `mail.ParseAnnouncement(canon, key, now)`. Only
     `ErrAnnouncementExpired` becomes null; every other failure is `bad_body`
     (`kinds.go:361-375`).
   - GC runs after the `teams`/`team_members` writes in the same tx (`kinds.go:194-199`,
     R14 L1). Peers inserted and collected in the same tx get no keys push (`kinds.go:200-214`).
   - A directly paired peer cannot be deleted by a roster. `GCIntroduced` selects
     `introduced_by IS NOT NULL` only (`internal/peers/store.go:434-440`), and `Introduce`
     only merges the mailbox for such a peer (`peers/store.go:402-406`).
3. **`team.join`.**
   - The lookup must match `peer_key = msg.from`, `used IS NULL` and `expires > now`
     (`kinds.go:444-446`). The invite is marked used on every outcome that finds it
     (`kinds.go:476`), so it is single use.
   - `team_full` is enforced (`kinds.go:472`).
   - A join from a current member bumps the epoch (R16 M2, `kinds.go:484-490`), but see
     C20-01.
   - A deleted, not-owned or missing team gives `inactive` (`kinds.go:462`). O-091 is still
     open on the invite side.
4. **Owner operations, state and audit.**
   - Every mutating IPC method calls `requireActive` and checks ownership
     (`internal/daemon/team.go:142-146,264-272,303,329,357`). The store methods themselves
     still do not check state (O-078, unchanged).
   - Audits: create (`store.go:245`), rename (`store.go:379`), delete (`store.go:408`),
     leave (`store.go:443`), remove (by the caller, `daemon/team.go:383`), member_add and
     member_leave (`kinds.go:514,578`), invite and join (`invite.go:41,72`).
   - A check-then-act gap between `requireActive` and the store call is reachable only by
     concurrent local IPC. For a member leave it at worst turns `removed` into `left`,
     which is harmless.
5. **Terminal safety.** Team names are `^[a-z0-9-]` (safe). Member and owner names come from
   peer cards and allow bidi/`Cf` (see C20-04).

## Findings

### C20-01 · Low · confirmed-test
- **Where:** `internal/team/kinds.go:484-490` (`applyJoin` already-member branch), `kinds.go:552-564` (`applyLeave`)
- **What goes wrong:** R16 M2 made a `team.join` from a key still on the roster succeed, for
  the case where the member was re-invited "before its `team.leave` arrived". That
  `team.leave` is outboxed and is delivered later. `applyLeave` then removes the member that
  has just rejoined, and it broadcasts only to the remaining members. The rejoined member is
  out on the owner's side while it believes it is `active`.
- **Scenario:** member J runs `team leave` while its `team.leave` sits in the outbox
  (backoff after a relay error, O-054/O-061, or a hostile relay withholding it). The owner
  re-invites J; J's `team.join` arrives and the epoch is bumped; J applies the roster and is
  `active`. The old `team.leave` arrives and the owner removes J (epoch +1). J gets nothing
  until a presence resync delivers the owner-only roster (`removed`). The user has to ask
  for another invite. `zz_review55_C20-01_test.go` fails: "members=1, rosters sent to
  joiner=1".
- **Spec:** `Docs/protocol/team.md` §`team.leave` (processes any leave from a current member)
  vs the intent of R16 M2 (the rejoin should win).
- **Fix direction:** make a leave older than the member's current `added` a no-op. For
  example, compare `msg.created` with `team_members.added`, or record the join's time and
  ignore earlier leaves.
- **Related:** R16 M2, O-054, O-061, O-080

### C20-02 · Low · confirmed-read
- **Where:** `internal/team/store.go:436` (`Leave` GC), `store.go:196` (`applyRoster` GC), `store.go:493` (`OwnerRemoved`); `internal/presence/sender.go:459-466` (`sendOne` → `MailboxPub`), `internal/daemon/mail.go:59-62`
- **What goes wrong:** the introduced peers leave the visible set because of the same
  transaction that deletes their `peers` row (GC). `OnMembersChanged` → `SyncVisibility`
  then calls `sendOne(peer, "offline")`, but `MailboxPub` finds no row and returns false,
  so no goodbye is sealed. Only directly paired peers (for example the owner) get one.
- **Scenario:** B is a member of A's team `x` with introduced member C. B runs
  `team leave x` (or is removed, or A dissolves `x`). C never receives B's goodbye and keeps
  showing B online until its window lapses, or until A's roster reaches C and C collects
  B.
- **Spec:** team.md §Operations Leave: "stop presence … (send `offline` first …), GC
  introduced peers"; presence.md §Sending "Goodbye … to peers that leave the visible set
  (… leaving a team, removal)"; team.md §Removing the owner "sends presence goodbyes as for
  Leave".
- **Fix direction:** compute the goodbye set and send it, or capture the mailbox pubs,
  before the GC transaction commits.
- **Related:** O-075, O-063

### C20-03 · Low · confirmed-read
- **Where:** `internal/daemon/team.go:332-340` (`team_leave`), `internal/team/store.go:417-446`
- **What goes wrong:** `Leave` commits `state = left` and GC before `team.leave` is
  submitted. If `Outbox.Submit` then fails (`ErrNoMailboxKey` for the owner, `ErrUnpaired`,
  or a DB error), the CLI gets an error, and a retry is refused with `team_inactive`
  (`requireActive`). No `team.leave` is ever sent. The owner keeps the member on its roster,
  and resync cannot repair this because the leaver's epoch equals the owner's.
- **Scenario:** a member with no mailbox key for its owner (O-038/O-071 state) runs
  `team leave`. The command fails, but the team shows `left`. The owner and the other
  members still list it until the owner runs `team remove` by hand.
- **Spec:** team.md §Operations Leave ("then submit `team.leave`").
- **Fix direction:** submit `team.leave` in the same transaction (`SubmitTx`), or allow
  `team_leave` on a `left` team to resend it.
- **Related:** O-088 (same pattern on join), O-095, O-078

### C20-04 · Low · confirmed-read
- **Where:** `cmd/agentnet/team.go:242,250` (`team show`), `cmd/agentnet/team.go:371-372` (`team join`); `internal/agentcard/agentcard.go:116-119` (`checkText` rejects only `IsControl`)
- **What goes wrong:** member and owner names are card names. `checkText` admits
  bidi/format characters (U+202E, U+2066…), and the CLI prints the names raw, without
  `termSafe` (`cmd/agentnet/fetch.go:267`).
- **Scenario:** a hostile member (or its owner's introduction) signs its card with a name
  such as `"‮ecila"`. `agentnet team show x` renders it as another member's name in
  the NAME column. The fingerprint column is still shown, which limits the spoof.
- **Spec:** rubric invariant 4 / threat model (paired peer must not drive our terminal with
  bidi); agent-card.md `name` "no control characters".
- **Fix direction:** pass names through `termSafe` in `team show`/`team join`, or reject
  `Cf` in `checkText`.
- **Related:** O-067 (name collisions), O-100 (same class for request text)

### C20-05 · Info · confirmed-read
- **Where:** `internal/team/store.go:681-726`
- **What goes wrong:** O-087 is only partly fixed. Expired `team_invites` and
  `team_pending_joins` rows are now pruned on every insert (`pruneInvites`,
  `prunePendingJoins`), so the tables are bounded by the insert rate. They are still not
  pruned "daily and at start" as team.md §Tables says. The rows stay only until the next
  invite or join. This is doc/spec drift with no security impact.
- **Scenario:** none beyond stale expired rows staying until the next invite.
- **Spec:** team.md §Tables "pruned daily and at start, in both invite tables".
- **Fix direction:** update team.md to say "on insert", or add the start-up prune.
- **Related:** O-087

## Assumptions and contracts

- `op.Msg.From` is the authenticated sender (mail signature verified, paired key) before
  `Apply` (`kinds.go:71,440,545`). The caller is `mail.Receiver`, via
  `internal/daemon/mail.go:245-247`. **unchecked** (C-mail chunk).
- `op.Msg.Body` is decoded with `UseNumber`, so `team.epoch` is a `json.Number`
  (`kinds.go:288`). **unchecked**. If a float arrived instead, every roster would be
  `bad_body`, which fails closed.
- `mail.ErrBadBody`-wrapped errors are acked `unsupported` and marked in `mail_seen`, while
  other errors are retried (`kinds.go:27`). **unchecked** (O-059 rule).
- `peers.Store.Introduce` never changes trust or card for `introduced_by IS NULL`
  (`peers/store.go:402-406`). **checked**.
- `GCIntroduced` deletes only `introduced_by IS NOT NULL` rows and fails its outbox rows
  (`peers/store.go:429-469`). **checked**.
- A direct v2 pairing or verify clears `introduced_by` (`peers/store.go:113,307`), so every
  member added via invite is a direct peer and `Delete`/`RemoveMember` need no GC.
  **checked**.
- `mail.ParseAnnouncement(canon, key, now)` verifies the signature by `key` and returns
  `ErrAnnouncementExpired` only for the check-5 time failure (`kinds.go:366-374`).
  **unchecked**.
- `agentcard.Verify` bounds card size and fields (`kinds.go:353`). **unchecked**
  (agent-card chunk).
- `peers.Manager.StartTagged`/`RedeemTagged` call `Completed` once, with `info.Peer` = the
  key the v2 exchange authenticated and `info.Lookup` in the normalised form
  `NormalizePairLookup` accepts (`invite.go:30-73`). **unchecked**.
- `presence.Sender.MaybeResync` calls `ResyncMember` only for teams owned by self
  (`presence/sender.go:609-611`). **checked**.
- `cascadeTeamRemoval` runs before the peer row is deleted (`daemon/trust.go:97-103`).
  **checked** by reading the call order only.

## Checked and fine

- The owner-trust check reads through the Apply tx (`kinds.go:76`). A missing row returns
  a retryable error (O-076, unchanged), which fails closed.
- `parseRosterBody` enforces exact member counts at the top level (1 and 6) and per member
  (4), plus `ValidID`, `ValidName`, `ParseKey` for owner and members, the epoch range
  `[1, 2^53)` (R16), the state enum and strict wire times (`kinds.go:262-390`).
- A dissolved roster, or one without self, never consumes a pending join (`kinds.go:87`,
  tests in `review16_test.go`).
- The `not_owner`, `owner_trust` and `not_invited` ignore paths return nil (acked) and
  write nothing (`kinds.go:79-121`).
- `applyJoin` refuses an invite for a team that is not owned by self as `inactive` rather
  than looping (`kinds.go:462`).
- `applyLeave` ignores the owner itself, unknown and inactive teams, and non-members
  (`kinds.go:552-561`).
- `team_remove` refuses self before resolving the peer (`daemon/team.go:273`).
  `removeMemberFromTeam` sends the owner-only roster to the removed key
  (`daemon/team.go:386`, `store.go:527-531`).
- `rosterSnapshot` reads in one tx and calls `Announcement` before the tx, so it avoids a
  single-connection deadlock (`store.go:544-551`). Members without a peers row are skipped
  (R16 M4).
- A removed recipient gets an owner-only roster: `rosterBody(full=false)` (`store.go:596-608`).
- `RecordInvite` replaces a reused lookup and resets `used` (`store.go:686-690`), as
  team.md §Tables says.
- Audit details carry keys, ids, names and epochs only. They carry no cards,
  announcements or codes (all `s.audited` calls in scope), as team.md §Audit requires.
- IPC `team_invite` checks owner, active and the member count before starting a pairing
  (`daemon/team.go:142-154`).
- O-078: the IPC layer gates every owner operation on `active`. Only the store-level check
  is still missing.
